# User Stories — Diet Recommender

A walk through how someone actually uses the app, from signing in to getting their meals and coming back for more. Each story is one thing the user wants to do, with how important it is to get done.

**Priority:** 

High = the app doesn't work without it. 

Medium = makes it genuinely good. 

Low = nice to have if there's time.

---

## Addressing the main pain points

**Who it's for.** People who eat toward a goal — a calorie or protein target — but don't have the time or patience to plan every meal around it. Gym-goers and anyone watching their protein, people with a dietary line they won't cross (vegetarian, pescatarian) or allergies to work around, and busy home cooks tired of asking "what do I even make today?" It's for the person who has the intent but keeps stalling on the daily decision.

**The problem.** Eating to a plan is a small decision you make over and over, and it's more annoying than it sounds. Every meal has to clear the same hurdles — does it fit my diet, does it dodge my allergies, does it land near my calories and protein — and doing that in your head three times a day is exactly why people give up. Most apps either hand you a rigid plan that ignores your restrictions and your taste, or leave all the tracking to you.

**How it solves it.** Ask a few things once — diet, allergies, calorie and protein targets — then hand back a full balanced day that already respects all of it. Every meal says why it's there, so you're not taking it on faith. Tap any meal for the actual recipe so you can cook it, save what you're missing to a shopping list, and rate what you like so tomorrow leans more toward your taste. Less deciding, more eating.

**Where it fits.** It's the planning layer between "I have a goal" and "I'm standing in the kitchen." Not a calorie tracker you log into after the fact, not a food-delivery app — a web app you open when you're deciding what to actually make. It picks up the part everyone finds tedious: turning your rules and goals into today's food.

**When you'd use it.** Day to day, at the moments the decision comes up — in the morning to see the day's meals, before a grocery run to grab the shopping list, and any time today's plan doesn't appeal and you want a fresh one. Rating happens naturally after you've tried something, and over a week or two the recommendations quietly become more "you."

## Getting in

**US-1 (High)**
As a new user, I want to create an account with my email and a password so my preferences and meals are saved and waiting for me next time.

**US-2 (High)**
As a returning user, I want to log back in and land right where I left off, with my goals and past picks intact.

**US-3 (High)**
As a user, I want to log out so my account stays mine on a shared computer.

**US-4 (Medium)**
As a user who forgot my password, I want a way to reset it so I'm not locked out of my own account.

---

## Telling the app about me

**US-5 (High)**
As a new user, I want to say whether I'm vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or pescatarian so I only ever get meals I'd actually eat.

**US-6 (High)**
As a user, I want to list what I'm allergic to so nothing with those ingredients ever shows up in my plan.

**US-7 (High)**
As a user, I want to enter my daily calorie and protein targets so the meals I'm given add up to what I'm aiming for.

---

## Getting my meals

**US-8 (High)**
As a user, I want a full day of meals recommended to me — breakfast, lunch, dinner, a snack — that fit my diet, dodge my allergies, and hit my targets, so I don't have to plan anything myself.

**US-9 (High)**
As a user, I want to tap a meal and see its ingredients and how to make it, so I can actually cook it.

**US-10 (High)**
As a user, I want each meal to tell me why it's here — fits my diet, right on calories, good protein, matches what I like — so I trust it instead of guessing.

**US-11 (High)**
As a user, I want to rate the meals I like, so that the more I use the app, the more it leans toward the food I actually enjoy. If I keep loving Italian, I want to see more of it.

---

## Shopping and coming back

**US-12 (Medium)**
As a user, when a recipe needs something I don't have, I want to tap it onto a shopping list so I remember to grab it next time I'm out.

**US-13 (Medium)**
As a user, I want to open my shopping list and tick things off as I buy them.

**US-14 (Medium)**
As a user, I want to save my favourite meals so I can find them again without hunting.

**US-15 (Medium)**
As a user, I want to update my diet, allergies, or goals whenever they change, and have my meals adjust to match.

**US-16 (Low)**
As a user, I want to ask for a new plan when I don't feel like today's, so I've always got options.
